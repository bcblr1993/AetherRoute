网络切换后自动恢复Recover after switching networks

  • 修复网线与 Wi-Fi 切换后 TUN 无法稳定恢复、需要手动重连的问题。同步实际出口接口，重建 DNS 与相关协议连接，避免重复重装路由。Fix TUN recovery after Ethernet/Wi-Fi changes by updating the active interface and rebuilding DNS and protocol connections without repeatedly reinstalling routes.
  • 断网或节点探测失败时保留已选代理，避免错误切到直连；无网络时保持恢复状态。Keep the selected proxy during outages and failed health checks, and keep the recovery state truthful while offline.
  • 修复 IPv6 hosts 地址族处理及 AAAA 映射，改善窄窗口布局与无障碍可读性。Correct IPv6 hosts address-family handling and AAAA mappings, with improved narrow-window layouts and accessibility.
