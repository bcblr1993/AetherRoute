AetherRoute 1.0.4

Build 2026091405 · 2026-09-14 · Apple silicon · macOS 15.0+

1.0.4 聚焦图标更新，网络行为沿用 1.0.3。请同时查看下方验证范围。Version 1.0.4 refreshes the icon; networking behavior is unchanged from 1.0.3. See validation coverage below.

全新的银白纸飞机Introducing Silver Flight

  • 设置 → 通用 → 外观新增跟随系统、浅色和深色主题，切换立即生效并记住选择。应用内图标随主题切换深浅底色，去掉外围蓝色光晕。General settings now offer System, Light and Dark themes with instant switching and persistence. In-app icons adapt to the theme, without the decorative blue glow.
  • 采用银白底、石墨色纸飞机与蓝色点缀，替换原有深色点阵图标。A porcelain-white tile, graphite paper plane and blue accent replace the dark dot-matrix icon.
  • 统一 Dock、Finder、应用内品牌标识及官网图标，提供 16–1024 像素资源。Consistent Dock, Finder, in-app and website branding with 16–1024 px assets.
  • 保留已有配置、订阅与 1.0.3 的断网恢复、异常退出自动重连行为。Existing profiles, subscriptions and the network recovery behavior from 1.0.3 are preserved.

验证范围Validation coverage

  • 485 项单元测试通过，图标尺寸、可重现性及 7 种 macOS 样式检查通过。485 unit tests passed; icon sizes, reproducibility and seven macOS renditions were checked.
  • Developer ID 签名、应用和 DMG 的 Apple 公证、票据装订与 Gatekeeper 校验通过。Developer ID signatures, Apple notarization of app and DMG, stapled tickets and Gatekeeper were verified.
  • 在 Tart macOS 26.6.1 中完成 1.0.3 → 1.0.4 覆盖升级，保留配置，并实测侧栏、首页和 Dock 的新图标。已实测主题即时切换、深浅图标同步和重启保存。An in-place upgrade from 1.0.3 to 1.0.4 in Tart macOS 26.6.1 preserved profiles. Sidebar, overview and Dock icons were visually checked. Instant theme changes, adaptive icons and persistence after relaunch were checked.

已知限制Known limitations

本次图标更新未重新执行全部协议矩阵、原生 IPv6、整机睡眠唤醒或 24 小时稳定性验证。1.0.3 已记录的 IPv6 HTTPS 与首次异常恢复延迟限制仍需留意，详见 1.0.3 版本说明。This icon update does not repeat the full protocol matrix, native IPv6, system sleep/wake or 24-hour stability validation. The IPv6 HTTPS and initial recovery-delay limitations recorded for 1.0.3 remain relevant.
