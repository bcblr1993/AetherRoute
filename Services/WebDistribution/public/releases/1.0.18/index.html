零配置透明国内与 Apple 服务加速引擎Zero-Config Domestic & Apple Acceleration Engine

  • 内存级无损配置优化：在不破坏用户磁盘原始配置的前提下，运行时自动注入 Apple 服务与国内高优规则，达成“国内直连丝滑、国外自动代理”。Lossless in-memory optimization dynamically injects Apple and domestic direct rules without mutating on-disk profiles.
  • Apple 服务 CDN 智能防护：针对 swcdn.apple.com 等系统更新服务智能过滤 fake-ip 劫持，国内 DNS 直接解析真实 Anycast IP，实测 34.9 MB/s 线速跑满带宽。Bypasses Fake-IP for Apple CDN domains, resolving to authentic domestic Anycast IPs for saturated line-rate downloads (34.9 MB/s verified).
  • 智能 DNS 分流：国内域名全自动映射至低延迟国内 DNS（223.5.5.5），杜绝海外 DNS 污染与解析延迟。Maps domestic domains to fast domestic upstream DNS (223.5.5.5), eliminating latency and poisoning.

网络切换防振荡与健壮解析Anti-Oscillation & Robust Parsing

  • 彻底根治应用层接收网络事件触发重复重置引起的无限振荡循环，保障休眠唤醒与 Wi-Fi 切换平稳自愈。Eliminated reassertion feedback loops during network path changes, ensuring smooth reconnection on wake and Wi-Fi handovers.
  • 全面兼容非缩进 YAML 列表项及不规则大小写/复数段落名，解析健壮性大幅增强。Fully compatible with non-indented list items and varied section case/plural headers in imported profiles.
